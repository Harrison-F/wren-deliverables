What this guide can—and cannot—say

This is a comprehensive first pass, not a census of every NYC club. “Recent” is evidence of a recent listing, not a guarantee of the next meeting. Same-day events may have ended. Confirm venue, registration and cost with the organizer.

Coverage, ranking & source limitations
